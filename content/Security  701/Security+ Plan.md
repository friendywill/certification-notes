For each week:
1. Read through each respective chapter in the study guide,
2. View Professor Messer's videos. From other YouTuber's who have passed the exam, his course was specifically recommended in their videos.
3. Use flashcards to remember key concepts.
4. Answer questions in the self-paced study guide.
5. Do any of the suggested labs in the textbook.
6. Anything in the activities mentioned below are extra activities on top of the aforementioned ones.
## Week 1: General Security Concepts (12%)

[[1.1 Compare and contrast various types of security controls.]]

[[1.2 Summarize fundamental security concepts.]]

[[1.3 Explain the importance of change management processes and their impact on security.]]

[[1.4 Explain the importance of using appropriate cryptographic solutions.]]

Activities: Review over some of the math I did in University for cryptography.

## Weeks 2-3: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations (22%)

[[2.1 Compare and contrast common threat actors and motivations.]]

[[2.2 Explain common threat vectors and attack surfaces.]]

[[2.3 Explain various types of vulnerabilities.]]

[[2.4 Analyze indicators of malicious activity.]]

[[2.5 Explain the purpose of mitigation techniques.]]

Activities: I may conduct some hands-on lab with these concepts, depending on what the book recommends. Review (Week 3): Spend 2 hours revisiting General Security Concepts from Week 1.

## Weeks 4-5: Security Architecture (18%)

[[3.1 Compare and contrast security implications of architecture models.]]

[[3.2 Apply security principles to secure enterprise infrastructure.]]

[[3.3 Concepts and strategies to protect data.]]

[[3.4 Importance of resilience and recovery.]]

Activities: Research best practices for securing architectures, set up mock network environments. Review (Week 5): Spend 2 hours reviewing Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations.

## Week 6: Focused Review Week

Review all previous domains: General Security Concepts, Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations, and Security Architecture.

Take a full-length practice exam and take note of weak areas.

Focus additional time on topics requiring improvement.

Activities: Practice tests, hands-on labs, and flashcard reviews.

## Weeks 7-8: Security Operations (28%)

[[4.1 Given a scenario, apply common security techniques to computing resources.]]

[[4.2 Explain the security implications of proper hardware, software, and data asset management.]]

[[4.3 Explain various activities associated with vulnerability management.]]

[[4.4 Explain security alerting and monitoring concepts and tools.]]

[[4.5 Given a scenario, modify enterprise capabilities to enhance security.]]

[[4.6 Identity and Access Management.]]

[[4.7 Automation and orchestration.]]

[[4.8 Incident response activities.]]

[[4.9 Using data sources for investigations.]]

Activities: Practical labs on SIEM, incident response simulations, read real-world case studies. Review (Week 8): Spend 2 hours revisiting Security Architecture.

## Week 9: Security Program Management and Oversight (20%)

[[5.1 Summarize elements of effective security governance.]]

[[5.2 Explain elements of the risk management process.]]

[[5.3 Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management.]]

[[5.4 Summarize elements of effective security compliance.]]

[[5.5 Explain types and purposes of audits and assessments.]]

[[5.6 Given a scenario, implement security awareness practices.]]

Activities: Research compliance frameworks, undertake a third-party risk assessment, flashcards for terms and concepts. Review: Dedicate 3 hours revisiting General Security Concepts and Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations.

## Week 10: Final Review and Exam Preparation

Revisit weak areas based on practice tests.

Complete at least two full-length practice exams.

Summarize key concepts and review flashcards daily.

Activities: Consolidate notes, focus on challenging areas, and reinforce key principles through flashcards and labs.
